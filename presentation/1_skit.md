# Gate Pe Gadbad: skit script (v2)

GateIQ | Team Eureka | About 4 minutes | Hinglish

## Cast

| Role | Played by | Who they are |
|---|---|---|
| **Selvam** | Meet | Truck driver. Tired, hungry, frustrated, basically a good guy. Truck TN39BX7712. |
| **Mehta ji** | Bhanwar | Owner of Indigo Loom Garments, the exporter. Cheerful, always "ho jayega", always on the phone. |
| **Sharma ji** | Love | Gate entry officer at Chennai port. Strict by duty, also stuck in the same mess. |

Horns and the "Freeze" narrator line: Kartikeya and Ayush from the side (they are not on stage).

Aditya is not in the skit: he checks the laptop, projector and the live site while the skit plays.

## Props

- A table and chair for Sharma ji, a laptop on the table (turned away from the audience until Scene 3).
- A stack of papers (A4 sheets with big headings: SHIPPING BILL, PACKING LIST, FORM-13, VGM, E-WAY BILL, LORRY RECEIPT). Put the real numbers on them in marker so they are readable from the front row (see the number table below).
- A cap for Selvam, a phone for Mehta ji.
- A paper sign saying STOP, held up by Sharma ji as the barrier.
- A wall clock or a watch for Selvam. Time never changes, but he keeps checking it.

## The numbers (match the demo, so the audience recognises them later)

These come from the demo case (scenario B, cotton T-shirts, Tiruppur to Chennai to Hamburg). Say them exactly like this.

| What | Paper A | Paper B |
|---|---|---|
| Container | Shipping bill: **GIQU 332017 3** | Packing list: **GIQU 332017 6** (fails the check digit) |
| Truck | Lorry receipt and e-seal: **TN39BX7712** | Form-13: **TN39BX7721** (two digits swapped) |
| E-way bill | Valid until **9 November** (three days ago) | Part B (vehicle) **empty** |
| VGM | Form-13: **7,820 kg** | VGM declaration: **7.82** under a kilogram heading |
| Cartons | Shipping bill and packing list: **600** | Lorry receipt: **580** |

The rewind in Scene 3 must not claim anything GateIQ does not do today: there are **no alerts or SMS**, and the **gate stage is not live yet**. Sharma ji only *views* the case.

## Scene 1: The Barrier  (about 1:15)

*(Selvam mimes driving, stops at the table. Sharma ji raises the STOP sign. Horns: none yet.)*

**Sharma ji:** Ruko ruko ruko! Gaadi side mein lagao. Kagaz dikhao.

*(Selvam hands over the stack.)*

**Selvam:** Ye lo sahab, sab ekdum sahi hai. Subah paanch baje Tiruppur se nikla hoon, seedha yahan aaya hoon.

*(Sharma ji reads slowly, holds up two papers, one in each hand.)*

**Sharma ji:** Shipping bill pe container GIQU 332017 3. Packing list pe GIQU 332017 6. Ek number ka chakkar hai, aur check digit bhi fail ho raha hai. Ye number system mein valid hi nahi hai.

**Selvam:** Arre sahab, mujhe kya pata! Mera kaam gaadi chalana hai, container ka number likhna nahi.

**Sharma ji:** *(next paper)* Form-13 pe truck number TN39BX7721. Tumhari gaadi?

**Selvam:** *(checks)* TN39BX7712! Wahi gaadi hai sahab, bas do number ulte ho gaye.

**Sharma ji:** Mere liye ulte number matlab doosri gaadi. Rules are rules, Selvam. Side mein jao.

*(Selvam steps aside, dials Mehta ji. First, soft horn from the side.)*

**Selvam:** Mehta sir! Gate pe rok diya. Container number bhi galat, truck number bhi.

*(Mehta ji picks up, relaxed, chai in hand.)*

**Mehta ji:** Arre Selvam, tension mat le. Typing mein ho gaya hoga. Ruk, office mein bolta hoon, sab theek ho jayega.

**Selvam:** Sir, ship ka cutoff chhe baje ka hai!

---

## Scene 2: One problem becomes five  (about 1:30)

*(Horns louder. Sharma ji picks up more papers.)*

**Sharma ji:** Aur sunna. Form-13 pe VGM 7,820 kilo. VGM declaration pe 7.82. Saat point aath do kilo ka container?! Kilo hai ya ton?

**Mehta ji:** *(on phone)* Wo ton hai, ton! Bas heading galat chhap gayi.

**Sharma ji:** Heading ki wajah se system mein 8 kilo ka container ghus raha hai. Aur ye e-way bill. Nau tareekh ko expire. Teen din pehle. Upar se Part B khaali, gaadi ka number hai hi nahi.

**Selvam:** Sahab, ye toh mujhe aaj subah pata chala!

**Sharma ji:** Lorry receipt pe 580 carton. Shipping bill pe 600. Bees carton kahan gaye?

**Mehta ji:** *(sheepish)* Wo... transporter ka LR hai, mera nahi. Mera sirf baaki sab hai.

**Selvam:** Matlab ek chhoti galti, aur sab ek doosre pe daal rahe ho!

*(Horns now loud. Mehta ji, phone to ear, hurried.)*

**Mehta ji:** Shipping line wale bhi phone kar rahe hain. Cutoff miss hua toh unka charge lagega, aur wo mujhe nahi, tumhe bolenge!

*(Selvam loses his temper. **Pause one full second** before he speaks: this is the emotional peak.)*

**Selvam:** Chaudah ghante se gaadi chala raha hoon. Diesel jala, neend gayi. Ek number ulta aur sab wapas? Ye system hai ya mazaak?

**Sharma ji:** *(tired, sits down)* Mujhe kya lagta hai, mujhe maza aa raha hai? Peeche chalis trucks ki line hai. Mere paas ye kagaz hain, baaki sab alag alag jagah pade hain. Kisi ko kuch pehle se pata hi nahi hota.

---

## Freeze  (about 0:15)

*(All three freeze mid-gesture. Horns stop on a single honk.)*

**Narrator (Kartikeya, from the side):** Ek typo. Truck wapas. Ship ka cutoff miss. Port pe jam. Aur ant mein detention aur demurrage. Galti ghar pe hui thi, saza gate pe mili. Ab dekhte hain wahi kahani... GateIQ ke saath.

*(Narrator: "Rewind." Everyone un-freezes, shuffles backwards two steps, turns around. The laptop on Sharma ji's table gets turned to face the audience.)*

---

## Scene 3: Rewind with GateIQ  (about 1:00)

*(Mehta ji sits at the table with the laptop, calm, a day earlier. No horns.)*

**Mehta ji:** *(to audience)* Kal dispatch se pehle maine GateIQ pe apna case khola. Checks wale tab mein dekha toh saari galtiyan lal. Container ke aage likha tha: "Did it mean GIQU 332017 3?" Maine packing list theek kar di. Truck 7721 ko 7712 kiya. VGM ki unit theek ki. E-way bill naya banwaya aur Part B mein gaadi ka number bhara. Transporter ne bhi apna LR theek kar diya, 600 carton.

*(He closes the laptop and gives a thumbs-up. Selvam walks up to the barrier calmly. Sharma ji is at the same table, laptop open facing him.)*

**Sharma ji:** Aao Selvam. Case khulla hai mere dashboard pe. *(scrolls)* Sab papers aa chuke hain. Container ek hi number, sab jagah. Truck wahi. E-way bill valid, gaadi ka number Part B mein. Koi flag nahi. Jao, aage badho.

**Selvam:** Bas itna? Sahab, aaj pehli baar gate pe muskurane ka mann kar raha hai!

*(All three step forward, face the audience.)*

**Sab:** Galti desk par pakdo, gate par nahi. Ye hai **GateIQ**.

*(Beat. Selvam, to the audience, pointing at the screen.)*

**Selvam:** Aur ab dekhiye, ye kaise kaam karta hai. Slides pehle, phir live.

*(All bow. Ayush walks on with the clicker to start the slides.)*

## Performance tips

- Build the horns slowly through Scenes 1 and 2. They give the bottleneck feeling without extra dialogue.
- Selvam should look more tired in every scene: slumped shoulders, wiping his face, checking his watch.
- Sharma ji is strict, not a villain. The point is that the system is broken, not the people.
- Mehta ji is likeable, not careless on purpose. He plays "ho jayega" with a smile until it stops working.
- Rehearse the freeze and the rewind so they are sharp. A clean freeze gets a laugh and sets up the reveal.
- Check every number above is readable on the props. The audience should be able to read "332017 3" against "332017 6".
- If the clock runs over, cut the lorry receipt line (580 vs 600) in Scene 2. It is the only cut that loses nothing the demo needs.

## Changes from the old skit

- Pintu the transporter's dispatcher is now **Mehta ji the exporter**, played by Bhanwar. Most of the mistakes are on the exporter's own papers, so he now owns them; the transporter's error (the 580 cartons) is a one-liner.
- Numbers switched to the demo case so the live website replays the skit.
- No more "GateIQ ne alert bheja": GateIQ has no alerts or SMS. The exporter fixes things by opening the Checks tab.
- The gate officer no longer asks for a Case ID. A gate officer is a view-only account added to the case, and sees it on his own dashboard.
- Scene 3 no longer says the gate approves entry: the gate stage is "not live yet". Sharma ji only sees clean papers.
