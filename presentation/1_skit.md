# Gate Pe Gadbad: skit script (v4, with emotions)

GateIQ | Team Eureka | About 3 minutes | Hinglish

## Cast

| Role | Played by | Who they are |
|---|---|---|
| **Raju** | Meet | Truck driver. Tired, hungry, frustrated, basically a good guy. Starts hopeful, ends broken, then relieved. |
| **Mehta ji** | Bhanwar | Owner of an export garment company. Cheerful, always on the phone, always "ho jayega". Starts carefree, ends guilty. |
| **Sharma ji** | Love | Gate entry officer at the port. Strict by duty, but a human being under pressure. |
| **Narrator** | Ayush | Speaks from the side during the freeze. Calm, almost like a news anchor. |

Horns from the side: Kartikeya and Aditya.

## The emotion map (read this first)

The words are short on purpose. The emotion is in how you say them.

| Scene | Raju | Sharma ji | Mehta ji |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The Barrier | Proud, hopeful, a little tired | Stern, official, slightly bored | Relaxed, singing-in-his-head |
| 2. One problem becomes many | Anxious, then pleading, then **explodes** | Rising stress, then **exhausted and honest** | Nervous laughing, then guilty silence |
| Freeze | (frozen, eyes wide) | (frozen, hand on forehead) | (frozen, phone still at his ear) |
| 3. Rewind | Stunned, then **pure relief** | Calm, even a little warm | Smug, proud |

Volume goes up through Scenes 1 and 2 (horns help), then **drops to silence** at the freeze. Silence is the loudest moment of the skit.

## Props

- A table and chair for Sharma ji, with a laptop turned away from the audience until Scene 3.
- A stack of papers with big headings (SHIPPING BILL, PACKING LIST, FORM-13, VGM, E-WAY BILL, LORRY RECEIPT).
- A cap for Raju, a phone for Mehta ji, a paper STOP sign, a watch or small clock.

No numbers are spoken. The audience should feel the mistakes, not read them.

## Scene 1: The Barrier  (about 0:50)

*(Raju walks in like a man on top of the world: shoulders back, humming, steering an invisible wheel, a little horn "pom pom" with his mouth. Sharma ji slams the STOP sign up.)*

**Sharma ji:** *(sharp, loud)* Ruko ruko ruko! *(Raju brakes, jerks forward, a small comic stumble.)* Gaadi side mein lagao. Kagaz dikhao.

**Raju:** *(big smile, handing over the stack proudly)* Ye lo sahab! Sab ekdum sahi hai. Subah se ek jagah nahi ruka, seedha aa raha hoon!

*(Sharma ji flips through slowly, face getting longer. He freezes on two papers. Slowly lifts one in each hand, side by side, and looks over them at Raju. Long beat.)*

**Sharma ji:** *(quiet, disbelieving)* Shipping bill pe container number ek... packing list pe thoda alag. *(he taps the page)* Aakhri ank hi nahi milta. *(louder)* Ye number system mein valid hi nahi hai!

**Raju:** *(smile slipping)* Arre... sahab, mujhe kya pata! Mera kaam gaadi chalana hai, number likhna nahi!

**Sharma ji:** *(another paper, eyebrow up)* Aur Form-13 pe truck ke aakhri do ank ulte hain. *(leans in)* Tumhari gaadi wahi hai?

**Raju:** *(laughs nervously, rubs his neck)* Wahi hai sahab... bas, ulte likh diye!

**Sharma ji:** *(firm, not angry, almost apologetic)* Mere liye ulte ank matlab doosri gaadi. Rules are rules, Raju. Side mein jao.

*(Raju's shoulders drop. He walks aside like a schoolboy sent out of class, pulls out his phone. Soft horns start. He dials, taps his foot.)*

**Raju:** *(hushed, panicked)* Mehta sir! Gate pe rok diya! Container bhi galat, truck bhi!

**Mehta ji:** *(lounging, slurping chai, totally relaxed)* Arre Raju, tension mat le yaar. Typing mein ho gaya hoga. Ho jayega!

**Raju:** *(glances at his watch, voice cracking)* Sir... ship ka cutoff aa raha hai...

---

## Scene 2: One problem becomes many  (about 0:40)

*(Horns louder, overlapping. Sharma ji picks up more papers with a heavy sigh. Raju paces, checking his watch every few seconds.)*

**Sharma ji:** *(exasperated, holding a paper out at arm's length)* Aur sunna! VGM ek paper pe hazaron kilo, doosre pe sirf kuch kilo! Ye kilo hai ya ton?!

**Mehta ji:** *(on phone, fake laugh)* Ha ha ha! Ton hai, ton! Bas heading galat chhap gayi!

**Sharma ji:** *(turns the paper, reads, stops)* E-way bill... kab ka expire ho chuka hai. *(louder)* Aur gaadi ka number bhi khaali!

**Mehta ji:** *(laugh dies. Small, guilty voice)* Wo... ho jayega... bas thoda time...

*(Silence for one second. Horns swell. Raju stops pacing. His hands start to shake. He crumples a corner of the cap in his fist.)*

**Raju:** *(low, trembling, then rising to a shout)* Poora din gaadi chalayi... diesel jala... neend gayi... *(cracks)* aur ek chhoti si galti se sab wapas?! *(slams his cap on the table)* Ye system hai ya mazaak?!

*(Everything stops. Horns cut. Sharma ji slowly puts down the papers, takes off an imaginary cap, rubs his face.)*

**Sharma ji:** *(quiet, tired, honest, almost to himself)* Mujhe maza aa raha hai kya, Raju? *(looks at the line behind him)* Peeche trucks ki line hai. Mere paas bas ye kagaz hain... aur kisi ko pehle se kuch pata hi nahi chalta.

---

## Freeze  (about 0:15)

*(Silence. All three hold their pose: Raju head down with both hands on the table, Sharma ji hand on forehead, Mehta ji phone still at his ear, mouth open. Hold three full seconds. Then one single, sad horn.)*

**Narrator (Ayush):** *(calm, slow, like a news anchor)* Ek typo. Truck wapas. Cutoff miss. Port pe jam. Galti ghar pe hui thi... saza gate pe mili. *(beat, then a small smile)* Ab dekhte hain wahi kahani... GateIQ ke saath. *(clap, bright)* Rewind!

*(Everyone un-freezes with a comic rewind: walk backwards two steps at double speed, spin around. Light, fast, playful: the mood flips. Sharma ji's laptop is turned to face the audience.)*

---

## Scene 3: Rewind with GateIQ  (about 0:50)

*(Mehta ji sits at the table with the laptop, a day earlier. No horns. He is calm and slightly smug, tapping keys. He looks up, to the audience.)*

**Mehta ji:** *(proud, like a man who has seen the light)* Kal dispatch se pehle maine GateIQ pe case khola. Checks mein saari galtiyan saaf dikh gayi! *(counts on his fingers)* Container ka number, truck ka number, VGM ki unit, e-way bill. Sab dispatch se pehle theek kar diya. *(a little bow)* Chai pee ke.

*(He closes the laptop with a click, thumbs-up. Raju strolls in, calm this time: whistling, cap on straight, relaxed. Sharma ji, laptop open, almost smiling.)*

**Sharma ji:** *(warm, genuinely pleased)* Aao Raju! Case mere dashboard pe khula hai. *(scrolls, nods)* Sab kagaz mil rahe hain. Koi flag nahi. Jao, aage badho.

**Raju:** *(stops, stunned, looks left, looks right, looks at the audience)* Bas itna?... *(it sinks in, he lights up, throws his arms wide)* Sahab, aaj pehli baar gate pe muskurane ka mann kar raha hai!

*(He hugs Sharma ji. Sharma ji stiffens, then pats him awkwardly. Mehta ji laughs. All three walk to the front, shoulder to shoulder.)*

**Sab:** *(together, big and happy)* Galti desk par pakdo, gate par nahi. Ye hai **GateIQ**!

*(All bow. Kartikeya walks on with the clicker to start the slides.)*

## Performance tips

- **Contrast is everything.** Scene 1 is light and funny, Scene 2 turns dark, the freeze is silent, Scene 3 is bright. If every scene is the same volume, it will feel like reading.
- **Build the horns slowly** through Scenes 1 and 2, then cut them dead at Raju's outburst and again at the freeze.
- **Raju's outburst** is the heart of the skit. Start low and shaky, not loud. Let the voice crack on "aur ek chhoti si galti". Then the cap slam.
- **Sharma ji is not a villain.** In Scene 2 he drops the strict act and tells the truth. The audience should feel for him too.
- **Mehta ji's laugh dies** mid-sentence in Scene 2. That moment is funnier and sadder than any line.
- **The freeze** needs three real seconds of stillness. Do not rush it.
- **The rewind** is a comic reset: fast, light, almost cartoon-like. This flips the mood.
- Raju looks more tired in every scene: slumped shoulders, wiping his face, checking his watch. In Scene 3 he stands tall again.
- Hold up the two papers side by side in Scene 1 so the audience sees the difference without hearing a number.
- If it runs long, cut Sharma ji's last line in Scene 2 after "Mujhe maza aa raha hai kya, Raju?".

## Behind the story (not spoken)

Same case as the demo: cotton T-shirts, Tiruppur to Chennai to Hamburg. Container number wrong on the packing list, truck number swapped on the Form-13, VGM in tonnes under a kilogram heading, e-way bill expired with no vehicle. The skit does not claim alerts or SMS (GateIQ has none), and the gate stage is not live, so Sharma ji only views the case.
