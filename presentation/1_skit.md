# Gate Pe Gadbad: skit script (v3)

GateIQ | Team Eureka | About 3 minutes | Hinglish

## Cast

| Role | Played by | Who they are |
|---|---|---|
| **Raju** | Meet | Truck driver. Tired, hungry, frustrated, basically a good guy. |
| **Mehta ji** | Bhanwar | Owner of an export garment company. Cheerful, always on the phone, always "ho jayega". |
| **Sharma ji** | Love | Gate entry officer at the port. Strict by duty, also stuck in the same mess. |
| **Narrator** | Ayush | Speaks from the side during the freeze. |

Horns from the side: Kartikeya and Aditya.

## Props

- A table and chair for Sharma ji, with a laptop turned away from the audience until Scene 3.
- A stack of papers with big headings (SHIPPING BILL, PACKING LIST, FORM-13, VGM, E-WAY BILL, LORRY RECEIPT).
- A cap for Raju, a phone for Mehta ji, a paper STOP sign.

The mistakes are the same ones the live demo shows, but no numbers are spoken: the audience should feel them, not read them.

---

## Scene 1: The Barrier  (about 0:50)

*(Raju mimes driving, stops at the table. Sharma ji raises the STOP sign.)*

**Sharma ji:** Ruko ruko! Gaadi side mein lagao. Kagaz dikhao.

**Raju:** Ye lo sahab, sab ekdum sahi hai. Subah se seedha chala aa raha hoon.

*(Sharma ji reads, holds two papers side by side.)*

**Sharma ji:** Shipping bill pe container number ek, packing list pe thoda alag. Aakhri ank hi nahi milta. Ye number system mein valid hi nahi hai.

**Raju:** Sahab, mujhe kya pata! Mera kaam gaadi chalana hai, number likhna nahi.

**Sharma ji:** Aur Form-13 pe truck ke aakhri do ank ulte hain. Tumhari gaadi wahi hai?

**Raju:** Wahi hai sahab, bas ulte likh diye!

**Sharma ji:** Mere liye ulte ank matlab doosri gaadi. Rules are rules. Side mein jao.

*(Raju steps aside and calls Mehta ji. Soft horns start.)*

**Raju:** Mehta sir! Gate pe rok diya. Container bhi galat, truck bhi.

**Mehta ji:** *(chai in hand)* Arre tension mat le. Typing mein ho gaya hoga. Ho jayega.

**Raju:** Sir, ship ka cutoff aa raha hai!

---

## Scene 2: One problem becomes many  (about 0:40)

*(Horns louder. Sharma ji picks up more papers.)*

**Sharma ji:** Aur sunna. VGM ek paper pe hazaron kilo, doosre pe sirf kuch kilo. Ye kilo hai ya ton?

**Mehta ji:** *(on phone)* Ton hai, ton! Heading galat chhap gayi.

**Sharma ji:** E-way bill kab ka expire ho chuka, aur gaadi ka number bhi khaali hai!

**Mehta ji:** *(sheepish)* Wo... ho jayega, bas thoda time...

*(Horns loud. Raju loses his temper. **Pause one second** before he speaks: this is the emotional peak.)*

**Raju:** Poora din gaadi chalayi, diesel jala, neend gayi. Ek chhoti si galti aur sab wapas? Ye system hai ya mazaak?

**Sharma ji:** *(tired, sits)* Mujhe maza aa raha hai kya? Peeche trucks ki line hai. Mere paas bas ye kagaz hain. Kisi ko pehle se kuch pata hi nahi chalta.

---

## Freeze  (about 0:15)

*(All three freeze mid-gesture. Horns stop on a single honk.)*

**Narrator (Ayush):** Ek typo. Truck wapas. Cutoff miss. Port pe jam. Galti ghar pe hui thi, saza gate pe mili. Ab dekhte hain wahi kahani... GateIQ ke saath. *(beat)* Rewind.

*(Everyone shuffles backwards two steps, turns around. Sharma ji's laptop is turned to face the audience.)*

---

## Scene 3: Rewind with GateIQ  (about 0:50)

*(Mehta ji sits at the table with the laptop, calm, a day earlier. No horns.)*

**Mehta ji:** *(to audience)* Kal dispatch se pehle maine GateIQ pe case khola. Checks mein saari galtiyan saaf dikh gayi. Container ka number, truck ka number, VGM ki unit, e-way bill. Sab dispatch se pehle theek kar diya.

*(He closes the laptop, thumbs-up. Raju walks up calmly. Sharma ji has the laptop open.)*

**Sharma ji:** Aao Raju. Case mere dashboard pe khula hai. Sab kagaz mil rahe hain, koi flag nahi. Jao, aage badho.

**Raju:** Bas itna? Sahab, aaj pehli baar gate pe muskurane ka mann kar raha hai!

*(All three step forward, face the audience.)*

**Sab:** Galti desk par pakdo, gate par nahi. Ye hai **GateIQ**.

*(All bow. Kartikeya walks on with the clicker to start the slides.)*

---

## Performance tips

- Build the horns slowly through Scenes 1 and 2.
- Raju looks more tired in every scene: slumped shoulders, wiping his face, checking his watch.
- Sharma ji is strict, not a villain. The system is broken, not the people.
- Mehta ji is likeable, not careless on purpose.
- Rehearse the freeze and rewind until they are sharp: that is the laugh.
- Hold up the two papers side by side in Scene 1 so the audience sees the difference without hearing a number.
- If it runs long, cut Sharma ji's last two lines in Scene 2.

## Behind the story (not spoken)

Same case as the demo: cotton T-shirts, Tiruppur to Chennai to Hamburg. Container number wrong on the packing list, truck number swapped on the Form-13, VGM in tonnes under a kilogram heading, e-way bill expired with no vehicle. The skit does not claim alerts or SMS (GateIQ has none), and the gate stage is not live, so Sharma ji only views the case.
